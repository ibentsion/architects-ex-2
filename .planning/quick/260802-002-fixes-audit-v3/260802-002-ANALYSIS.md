# Quick task 260802-002 — agent fixes, refgen v3, retrieval hit-rate audit

Date: 2026-08-02. Follow-ups from the 260801-004 failure analysis.

## T1 — Agent engine fixes

**Unfiltered retry** (`642baee`). A category-filtered retrieval that gates to
zero now retries the same query with no filter, in the prefetch and in the
loop's `retrieve` tool; the trace records `retried_unfiltered`. The classifier's
tags are 76-88% accurate, and a single wrong tag used to turn into a hard
refusal — the top failure mode in the 260801-004 eval (~24 empty-pool refusals).

**Difficulty-aware synthesis routing** (`f7ac01c`). The classifier now also
returns `difficulty` (easy|medium|hard; `medium` on any parse failure), and the
engine picks the synthesis model from it:

| query | synthesis model |
|---|---|
| easy/medium, single category | `harness.fast_synthesis_model` (gpt-oss-120b low) |
| hard, or multi-category, or needs_calculation, or dependent | `generation.model` (DeepSeek-V4-Pro) |

The split follows the 260801-004 committee evidence: the two models tie on
easy/medium and DeepSeek only pulls ahead on hard (6.44 vs 4.12). The fast
generator inherits the generation block's answer contract (prompt variant,
temperature, citation retry) — only model, max_tokens and extra_params differ,
so routing can never change what an answer is allowed to look like.
`fast_synthesis_model: null` disables the routing entirely.

Not yet measured end-to-end: the next agent eval should read
`trace[].fast_synthesis` to attribute scores per route.

## T2 — Reference set v3 (`14862be`)

`refgen.run --profile v3`: three multi-source items per category from a
calculation-biased prompt (rule-plus-figure and two-figures-combined first,
operands and result spelled out in the ground truth), holding out v1 and v2.
`--v1` became `--holdout` (multiple files) in both refgen CLIs.

**17 items of 36**, over 10 of 12 categories (apartment and dental produced
none), 0 audit problems, 16/17 answers carry a number. 301 rejections, of which
**171 were `needs_both`** — one of the two drawn pages answered the question by
itself. Three multi-source items per category is more than most categories have
distinct-subject page pairs for; the gate refusing them is the gate working.
Raising `MAX_PAIRS` above 8 would buy a few more items at linear cost.

## T3 — Retrieval hit-rate audit (`be9c22b`, run `retrieval-audit-20260802T043232Z`)

Every reference question through the real retriever (fuse, then rerank+gate; no
category filter, no generation), each ground-truth source group filed under the
furthest stage it reached. 4 arms x 2 datasets, on the L40S node.

| arm | set | groups | gated | not_gated | not_retrieved | missing_from_index | group hit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| pdf-per_table | v1 | 58 | 23 | 10 | 24 | 1 | 39.7% |
| pdf-per_table | v2 | 117 | 53 | 23 | 40 | 1 | **45.3%** |
| pdf-per_page | v1 | 58 | 26 | 13 | 18 | 1 | **44.8%** |
| pdf-per_page | v2 | 117 | 42 | 32 | 43 | 0 | 35.9% |
| markdown-per_table | v1 | 58 | 22 | 12 | 23 | 1 | 37.9% |
| markdown-per_table | v2 | 117 | 44 | 24 | 44 | 5 | 37.6% |
| markdown-per_page | v1 | 58 | 23 | 15 | 19 | 1 | 39.7% |
| markdown-per_page | v2 | 117 | 40 | 32 | 43 | 2 | 34.2% |

Pooled over both sets (175 groups per arm):

| arm | gated | not_gated | not_retrieved | missing_from_index |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| pdf-per_table | **43.4%** | 18.9% | 36.6% | 1.1% |
| pdf-per_page | 38.9% | 25.7% | 34.9% | 0.6% |
| markdown-per_table | 37.7% | 20.6% | 38.3% | 3.4% |
| markdown-per_page | 36.0% | 26.9% | 35.4% | 1.7% |

### What it says

1. **The corpus is not the problem.** `missing_from_index` is 0.6-3.4%. Whatever
   is losing ground-truth pages, it is not parsing or ingest coverage — so
   re-parsing work has almost no headroom to recover.
2. **First-stage recall is the bottleneck.** 35-38% of ground-truth pages are in
   the index and never enter the dense∪sparse top-20 candidate pool. That is
   twice the loss of the rerank gate (19-27%) and thirty times the corpus gap.
   The cheapest lever available: `dense_top_k`/`sparse_top_k` are 20, and rerank
   costs 9 ms/pair on the L40S — raising both to 50-100 is affordable and is the
   only change that can address `not_retrieved` at all.
3. **20-30% of v2 questions gate to nothing even unfiltered** (pdf-per_table:
   22/109; markdown-per_page: 33/109). T1's unfiltered retry removes the
   category filter as a cause of refusals, but this is the floor underneath it —
   these questions refuse because the pipeline finds nothing it rates relevant,
   not because a tag was wrong.
4. **Docling beats markdown at the retrieval level too**, on every arm and both
   sets (pooled 43.4% vs 37.7% at per_table). This is the same verdict
   260801-002 reached end-to-end, now with a mechanism: markdown indexes ~180
   fewer pages (3642 vs 3824) and carries 3-5x the `missing_from_index`. Caveat:
   both reference sets were authored from Docling parses, so pages Docling
   produced and markdown did not are counted against markdown by construction.
5. **per_table over per_page** pooled (43.4% vs 38.9%), and the split is
   informative: per_page loses more at the gate (25.7% vs 18.9%), which is what
   whole-page chunks do to a cross-encoder — one relevant paragraph diluted by a
   page of unrelated text scores lower. v1 alone prefers per_page (44.8% vs
   39.7%); v2, three times the size, prefers per_table by 9.4 points.
6. **Multi-source questions are barely served at all.** In the production arm,
   v2's 8 multi-source questions have **0 with both ground-truth pages gated**
   (4 of 17 groups gated). No synthesis model can answer those completely. This
   is the strongest argument for the v3 set: it measures exactly the case the
   pipeline is worst at.

Reading caveat: a `not_retrieved` group does not mean the answer is wrong — the
fact may be stated on another page the retriever did find. This audit measures
ground-truth page recall, which is a diagnostic, not a score.

### Next levers, in order of expected value

1. Raise `dense_top_k`/`sparse_top_k` (20 -> 50-100) and re-run this audit; it
   is the only knob that touches the 36% `not_retrieved` bucket.
2. Re-tune `gate_threshold`/`top_n` against the `not_gated` bucket, per chunker
   (per_page needs a lower threshold than per_table).
3. Only then revisit the parse: the corpus gap is ~1%.
