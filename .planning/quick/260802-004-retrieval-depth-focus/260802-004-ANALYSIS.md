# Quick task 260802-004 — depth, filters, and what actually reaches generation

Date: 2026-08-02 · Run `retrieval-depth-20260802T194610Z` · validation half
(77 answerable questions, 84 ground-truth source groups) · production arm
`pdf-per_table` unless stated · retrieval only, no generation, no judges, no
classification calls.

## Answer to the question that started this

**The 260802-002 audit did not include the unfiltered-retry fix, and did not
need to**: it ran with no category filter at all, so its numbers were already
the fix's ceiling. What it could not show is what classification *costs*, which
is what this run measures.

## Headline: the binding constraint is the reranker and its gate, not recall

| pool depth | what the pool contains | what reaches generation (top 6, gate 0.35) |
|---|---|---|
| K=20 (production) | 63% of ground-truth groups | 44.0% |
| K=100 | **83%** | **44.0%** |

Deepening the candidate pool from 20 to 100 adds 20 points of *reachable*
evidence and **delivers 3 groups**: of the 17 groups whose page sits at fused
rank 21-100, exactly 3 survive rerank + gate. The other 14 are outranked by
material the reranker prefers.

This reverses 260802-002's first recommendation. Raising `top_k` was the
cheapest lever, and it is nearly worthless on its own.

Where the retrieved-but-lost groups actually die (mode `none`, K=100):

| | groups |
|---|---|
| below the 0.35 gate | **20** |
| above the gate, ranked below top 6 | 13 |
| not in a 100-deep pool at all | 14 |
| reached generation | 37 |

Median rerank score of a retrieved-but-not-gated ground-truth page: **0.201**.
The gate was tuned to suppress bad evidence; it is currently suppressing more
correct evidence than depth is losing.

### What the two downstream knobs would buy (same run, re-scored)

Coverage of the 84 groups at K=100:

| gate \ top_n | 6 | 10 | 20 | 30 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.35 (production) | 44.0% | 47.6% | 57.1% | 58.3% |
| 0.20 | 45.2% | 51.2% | 61.9% | 63.1% |
| 0.10 | 45.2% | 51.2% | 65.5% | 66.7% |
| 0.00 | 50.0% | 57.1% | 76.2% | 78.6% |

`top_n` is worth roughly three times `top_k`, and the gate is worth as much
again. **But this table only shows the upside.** The gate is what produces the
"not enough information" refusal, and the audit skips unanswerable questions by
construction (they have no ground-truth page), so the cost of loosening it is
invisible here. The validation half carries 7 unanswerable items and the
holdout 5; deciding these two numbers needs a generation run that is scored on
those, not this table.

## Classification: currently a coin flip, worth ~11 points if fixed

Paired per group, same questions (n=84):

| comparison | gained | lost | net |
|---|---|---|---|
| none → predicted (`merged-B` tags) | +7 | −6 | **+1** |
| none → gold (perfect filter) | +9 | −0 | **+9** |
| predicted → gold | +8 | −0 | +8 |

Filtering by the *right* category never loses a ground-truth page (+9/−0, a
strict dominance — sign test p ≈ 0.002); filtering by the classifier's category
gains and loses in equal measure. 260802-003 moved tag accuracy from 73% to 81%
correct, and at the retrieval level that is still indistinguishable from not
classifying at all.

So "try without classification" and "keep classification" are, today, the same
system. The 11 points sit in the gap between `predicted` and `gold`, which is
exactly what 260802-003's follow-up (category-**set** filters) attacks.

### The unfiltered retry is worth +3.6 points, and no confidence score is needed

Simulating "use the filtered pool, fall back to unfiltered when its best rerank
score is below τ":

| τ | questions falling back | groups covered |
|---|---|---|
| — (always filtered) | 0/77 | 38/84 = 45.2% |
| **0.35** | 14/77 | **41/84 = 48.8%** |
| 0.50 | 23/77 | 41/84 = 48.8% |
| 0.70 | 29/77 | 41/84 = 48.8% |
| 0.90 | 51/77 | 37/84 = 44.0% |
| always unfiltered | 77/77 | 37/84 = 44.0% |

τ = 0.35 **is** the shipped fix: "nothing clears the gate under the filter" and
"the filtered retrieval returned an empty pool" are the same event. So the
260802-002 retry is worth +3 groups over always-filtering and +4 over never
filtering — it is what makes classification net-positive at all. The plateau
from 0.35 to 0.70 means the policy is insensitive to where exactly the
threshold sits, and every τ above it degrades toward never-filtering.

**Recommendation: do not add a classification confidence score.** The best
policy in this family is already in production, and the flat region says a
better-calibrated trigger has nothing to earn.

Caveat: production applies the filter and the retry per sub-question; this
simulation is per question. Multi-sub-question queries will differ in detail.

## Where an extend/focus phase would have signal

By difficulty (mode `none`, K=100):

| difficulty | groups | pool depth for 50% | K=20 | K=100 | reaches generation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| easy | 25 | 11 | 68% | 88% | 64% |
| medium | 25 | 4 | 72% | 96% | 48% |
| hard | 34 | 11 | 53% | 71% | **26%** |

By kind: `multi_source` is 17% at **every** depth (6 groups) — those pages are
not retrieved at all, so no focus/extend policy touches them.

By category, the two failure signatures are cleanly separated:

| category | groups | K=100 | reaches generation | reading |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| loss-of-working-ability | 6 | 100% | 17% | rerank-bound |
| dental | 4 | 100% | 25% | rerank-bound |
| mortgage | 7 | 100% | 29% | rerank-bound |
| long-term-care | 4 | 100% | 50% | rerank-bound |
| business | 10 | **40%** | 30% | recall-bound |
| life | 9 | 67% | 44% | recall-bound |
| diseases-disabilities | 5 | 100% | 80% | fine |

Categories where everything is retrieved and then thrown away (extend does
nothing; the reranker is the problem) are distinguishable from categories where
retrieval never finds the page (business — the largest category at 4,071 chunks
— cannot reach 60% of its ground truth at any depth measured).

That is the shape of an extend/focus decision, and it is the honest state of
the evidence: **4-10 groups per category is a hypothesis generator, not a
setting**. The v3 set and the holdout half exist to test it.

## What to do next, in order

1. **Re-tune `top_n` and `gate_threshold` on the validation half with
   generation and judging**, scored on the unanswerable items too. This audit
   says the upside is 13-32 points of evidence coverage; only a scored run says
   what it costs in fabricated answers. Confirm the chosen pair on the holdout.
2. **Category-set filters** (260802-003's follow-up): the `predicted → gold`
   gap is +8/−0, and family-level sets are how that gap closes.
3. **`top_k` stays at 20** until (1) lands. Raising it is free ceiling and
   almost zero delivery while the gate is where it is.
4. Multi-source and `business` are a retrieval-recall problem that none of the
   above touches — a different lever (query expansion per sub-question, or the
   agent's second retrieve hop) and a separate measurement.
