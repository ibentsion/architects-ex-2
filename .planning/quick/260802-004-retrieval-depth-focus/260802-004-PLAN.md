# Quick Task 260802-004 — depth-to-cover, filter modes, extend/focus

## Why now

260802-002's audit ran **unfiltered** (no category filter anywhere), so its 36%
`not_retrieved` / 19-27% `not_gated` split is already the classifier-free
ceiling — tuning `top_k` against it would be tuning against a number that has
never seen a filter. 260802-003 separately moved tag accuracy to 81% correct /
14% wrong but measured only the tag, never its effect on retrieved evidence.
Neither answers the two questions that decide what to do next:

1. Does filtering by category help or hurt *coverage*, versus not classifying
   at all — and what would a confidence-gated filter buy?
2. How deep does retrieval have to go before a question's ground-truth pages
   are in hand, and does that depth differ by difficulty and category?

(2) is what makes an extend/focus phase decidable rather than guessed.

## T1 — Validation split

`evalharness/split.py` (new) → **`ref_q_validation_set_v1.jsonl`**: a seeded 50%
sample of v1 + v2 pooled, stratified by (set, domain, difficulty, kind) so the
tail cells survive; complement written to `ref_q_holdout_set_v1.jsonl` so a
policy tuned on the validation half can be confirmed on the other half instead
of on the data that produced it. JSONL, item schema unchanged, both committed.
n ≈ 85 / 84 out of 169 (v1 48 standard; v2 101 standard + 8 multi_source + 12
unanswerable). Unanswerable items stay in the split — they carry no ground-truth
page and are skipped by the depth analysis, but any focus/extend policy has to
be judged on them too (extending retrieval on a question with no answer is how
a refusal turns into a fabrication).

## T2 — Depth to cover (extend `evalharness/retrieval_audit.py`)

Retrieve with a deep pool (`--deep-k`, default 100 dense + 100 sparse) and
record, per ground-truth source group:

- `fused_rank` — best rank of any chunk of that page in the fused list (the
  answer to "how many chunks would we have had to keep");
- `rerank_rank` — its rank after the cross-encoder scores the whole deep pool;
- `rerank_score` — so a page that ranks #1 but sits under the 0.35 gate is
  distinguishable from one that ranks #80;
- `n_chunks_same_page` — how many chunks that page contributes (per_table
  averages 3.2/page, per_page 1.2, so "chunks needed" is not "pages needed").

Report per difficulty × category × kind: the `top_k` (pre-rerank) and `top_n`
(post-rerank) at which 50 / 80 / 90% of groups are covered, plus the share
lost to the gate rather than to depth. Production today is `top_k=20,
top_n=6`; the deliverable is the curve those two numbers sit on.

## T3 — Filter modes: none vs predicted vs gold

Same deep run, three modes (`--filter-mode`):

- `none` — today's audit, and the unfiltered-retry ceiling;
- `predicted` — the classifier's `effective_filter`, read from
  `eval_results/classify-merge-20260802T050208Z/merged-B/predictions.jsonl`
  (260802-003's best arm) — **no new LLM calls**;
- `gold` — the item's own `domain`, the filter ceiling.

This is the "try without classification at all" arm, measured on coverage
rather than on tag accuracy: `gold − none` is what a perfect classifier could
buy, `predicted − none` is what this one actually buys, and `gold − predicted`
is what the remaining 14% of wrong filters cost.

## T4 — Confidence and the extend/focus decision

From T2/T3 on the validation half only:

- **Confidence proxy available at query time**: the share of the top-10
  unfiltered hits that fall in the predicted category (260802-003 already
  builds this hint; `hint-vote` abstained on 43% of questions using it), plus
  the tag count. Evaluate offline: "filter only when agreement ≥ X" against
  always-filter and never-filter, on coverage.
- **Extend trigger**: best rerank score / count above the gate as a predictor
  of an uncovered question — i.e. can the pipeline tell, without the ground
  truth, that it should go deeper?
- Output is a recommendation with numbers: per-difficulty `top_k`/`top_n`, a
  filter policy, and whether an extend/focus phase clears its own bar. Engine
  implementation is explicitly **out of scope** here — 260802-003's
  category-set filter touches the same code, and neither should be written
  before this task says which of them the data supports.

this directory, STATE row, commits pushed.
