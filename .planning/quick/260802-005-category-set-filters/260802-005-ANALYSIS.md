# Quick task 260802-005 — category-set filters and K=100

Date: 2026-08-02 · Run `retrieval-depth-20260802T202134Z` · validation half
(77 answerable questions, 84 ground-truth source groups) · `pdf-per_table`,
K=100 · retrieval only, no LLM calls of any kind.

## Result: set filters fix the recall damage and deliver nothing

| filter | reaches generation | vs `none`, paired | pool@20 | pool@100 | questions with an empty pool |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| none | 44.0% (37) | — | 63% | 83% | 8 |
| **gold** | **54.8% (46)** | **+9 / −0** | 76% | 87% | 11 |
| gold-family | 47.6% (40) | +3 / −0 | 69% | 87% | 8 |
| predicted (`single`, today) | 45.2% (38) | +7 / −6 | 64% | 75% | 14 |
| predicted-set | 45.2% (38) | +7 / −6 | 63% | 74% | 15 |
| predicted-family | 45.2% (38) | +3 / −2 | 67% | **85%** | 9 |

Family expansion does exactly what it was built to do and it is not enough:

- **It repairs the recall a wrong tag destroys.** A single predicted tag pushes
  pool@100 down from 83% (unfiltered) to 75%; widening it to the family brings
  that back to 85%. The 8 points a wrong filter was hiding are recovered.
- **None of it converts.** All three predicted modes deliver 38 of 84 groups.
  The extra evidence lands in the pool and the reranker does not put it in the
  top 6 — the same wall 260802-004 hit with depth.
- **Widening a correct filter costs.** `gold-family` is 6 groups worse than
  `gold` (47.6% vs 54.8%): the family dilutes a filter that was already right,
  and that loss cancels the gain on the wrong ones. `predicted-family` vs
  `predicted` is +4/−4.

So the only filter that helps is the one we cannot have. `gold` is +9/−0 —
strictly better, never worse — and 260802-003 showed the gap to it is not a
prompt problem: 8 of the 23 wrong tags are `mortgage → apartment` questions
that *are* apartment questions, filed under mortgage because that is where
Harel's document lives.

## Where the policies diverge: looser gate settings

Same run, re-scored at settings 260802-004 recommended exploring:

| filter | N=6 / gate .35 (today) | N=10 / .20 | N=20 / .10 | N=20 / .00 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| none | 44.0% | 51.2% | **65.5%** | 76.2% |
| gold | 54.8% | 59.5% | 69.0% | 81.0% |
| predicted (`single`) | 45.2% | 50.0% | 58.3% | 69.0% |
| predicted-set | 45.2% | 48.8% | 57.1% | 67.9% |
| **predicted-family** | 45.2% | 53.6% | 64.3% | **76.2%** |

At production's gate every policy is a tie. Loosen it and `single`/`set` fall
7 points behind not filtering at all, while `family` tracks `none` exactly.
Whatever the gate ends up being, the family policy is the one that never loses.

## Decisions

1. **`harness.category_filter: family`.** A tie with `none` on coverage, half
   the empty pools of `single` (9 vs 14 — fewer unfiltered retries, so lower
   latency), and the only predicted policy that survives a looser gate. `none`
   is an equally defensible choice on today's numbers; `family` is chosen
   because it costs nothing and keeps the category signal usable if the
   taxonomy problem is ever fixed.
2. **K=100 stays**, with the cost stated plainly: it is 100 cross-encoder pairs
   per query (~0.9 s on an L40S) instead of 20, and at today's `top_n`/gate it
   buys nothing. It is a *precondition* — at N=20/gate 0.1 a 100-deep pool gives
   65.5% against a 20-deep pool's 53.6%. If the gate re-tune does not happen
   before the final test, reverting `dense_top_k`/`sparse_top_k` to 20 is one
   line and costs nothing measurable.
3. **The one lever left is `top_n` and `gate_threshold`**, worth 20-32 points of
   evidence coverage across every filter policy. It cannot be decided from
   retrieval alone: the gate is what produces the refusal, and loosening it
   trades coverage for fabrication on the unanswerable items. That is a scored
   generation run on the validation half, and it is the last thing standing
   between here and the final test.

## Caveats

- 84 groups. Differences under ~5 groups (6 points) are noise; the claims above
  rest on paired counts, and the only strict dominance is `gold` (+9/−0).
- The filter is replayed per question from `merged-B`'s tags; production filters
  per sub-question, so multi-part queries differ in detail.
- The holdout half (85 items) is still unspent.
